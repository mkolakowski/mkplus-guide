# Help

- **Something not working, or a page here wrong?** In MK Plus, go to
  **Settings → Provide Feedback**. You can attach pictures and, if you choose,
  the app's Activity Log.
- **What changed in the latest update?** See [What's New](whats-new.html), or
  **Settings → Changelog** in the app for every detail.
- **What does MK Plus do with your information?** See the
  [Privacy Policy](privacy/).
- **Which open-source projects does it use?** See
  [Open-source libraries](open-source/).
