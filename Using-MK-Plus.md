# Use the app

Once a server's added, these pages show how to find your way round the app:
your Home, browsing, watching, listening and reading, privacy, and what's
different on each device. Each setting has its own page under
[Settings](Settings.html).

Screens that look different on each phone or iPad are shown side by side,
each labelled with its model.
