MK Plus runs on every iPhone and on iPad, and it's the same app on all of them. It fits itself to the screen it's on. The iPhone Duo, which folds, and the iPad, which is big, get a few extra layouts and a few settings of their own. This page says what changes, and which setting changes it.

## Any iPhone, small or large

- The tab bar floats across the bottom. Turn your iPhone on its side and a video fills the screen, and a book shows two pages side by side. See [Watching video](Video-Player.html) and [Reading](Reader.html#two-pages-at-once).
- A small iPhone, such as the iPhone 17e, gets the same app and the same settings. There's nothing that's only for small iPhones. If the bar feels big, turn off Settings → Home Screen → **Tab Names** for symbols only, in a smaller bar. **Fit Shelves to the Screen** in the same place keeps Home's covers from running past the edge. In an EPUB, **Text Size** and **Margins** are in Reading Options.

## The iPhone Duo

The Duo folds, and the app notices how it's held: closed, open, and partly folded. Settings → Home Screen, File Browsing, Video Playback, Reading, Catalogs and Image Viewer each have a few switches that only show on a Duo. Some are advanced, so turn on **Show Advanced Settings** to see all of them.

### Folded, on the front screen

- The tab bar stands up down the edge, in the strip beside the screen, upright or held on its side. Held on its side with the camera at the upper left, **Tab Bar Beside the Camera** (Home Screen) puts it down the left edge, next to the camera.
- Home's buttons and names can move. With **Home Names on the Right** on, the name of the Home you're on runs sideways down the strip, and you tap it for the menu of Homes. **Edit Between + and Settings**, **Buttons on the Right on Its Side** and **Fade Shelves at the Edge** move more of it. They're in an **iPhone Duo** section of Settings → Home Screen.
- In a folder, **Compact Cards** (File Browsing) makes the Cards layout half as tall, so twice as many fit. **Filter and Play Buttons** (Home Screen) gives a folder's filter and **Play All** their own buttons down the side of the bar. Off, they share one **Options** menu. **More Menu When Short** tucks the least-used buttons into a **More** menu where they don't all fit.
- A card (a folder or movie that slides up) sits beside the strip. Settings → File Browsing → **Cards on the iPhone Duo**, with Show Advanced Settings on, lets each posture use **Card** or **Full Screen**.
- A video keeps its controls clear of the strip. A book shows one page.
- On a book server's series page, **Series Buttons in the Strip** (Settings → Catalogs) moves Mark as Read, Auto-Download and Home into the strip.

### Unfolded

- Home can be **One Column**, **Two Columns** or **Two Homes Side by Side**. Upright, it's **Two Columns** to start with. On its side it can also be **In a Card**, a card down the middle, and it is to start with. Pick them in Settings → Home Screen → **Unfolded, Upright** and **Unfolded, on Its Side**. **Header in the Top Area** and **Content Under the Bottom Bar** change how upright Home meets the screen's edges.
- **Home Buttons in the Tab Bar** (Home Screen) moves **+** and **Edit** into the tab bar, so they're within reach.
- Settings keeps its list of pages on the left and the page you pick on the right, with the Duo on its side. Switch it with **Two Columns on the Unfolded Duo**. See [Settings](Settings.html#settings-display).
- A book shows two pages side by side on its side. Upright it shows one, unless **Two Pages When Upright** is on in Settings → Reading → **Page Layout**.
- A video has its own layout, and the **Media Controls** editor adds two buttons at the top, **Loadout 1** and **Loadout 2**. Tap an empty one to save your layout, tap a saved one to use it, and hold one to save over it or clear it. See [Lay the controls out your way](Video-Player.html#lay-the-controls-out-your-way).
- **Open in the Other Half** puts a second folder in the other half of the screen: beside the first on its side, below it upright. Press and hold a saved server on Home, or select one folder, and choose it. **Close Half** goes back to one. Drag files from one half onto a folder in the other to copy them. With **Hold to Drag When Split** on, a press and hold picks a file up to drag, instead of starting select mode. These are in Settings → File Browsing.
- On a book server's series page, with the Duo on its side, **Series Details Beside the List** (Settings → Catalogs) puts the series' details in a column beside its books.

### Partly folded, like a book

With the Duo on its side and bent so the fold runs top to bottom, the two halves sit side by side.

- Home: **Clear of the Fold** puts **Two Columns** and **Two Homes Side by Side** either side of the fold, and shows **In a Card** as **Two Columns**.
- Settings moves into one half, as one column.
- A video: **Book Layout** (Video Playback → **Gestures & Buttons**) puts the picture in one half and the controls in the other.
- A book: **Pages Either Side of the Fold** (Reading → **Page Layout**) leaves a gap as wide as the fold between two pages.
- Pictures: **Two Side by Side When Folded** (Image Viewer) shows two pictures at once, one each side of the fold, and moves on two at a time.
- Browsing: **Split When Folded** puts a folder in one half and your saved servers in the other when you fold the Duo from flat. **From Home Too** does it from Home as well.

### Partly folded, like a laptop

Upright, and bent so the fold runs across, the Duo can stand on a table. **Tabletop Layout** (Video Playback → **Gestures & Buttons**) puts the video on the half that stands up, with the controls on the half resting on the table. A split browser puts one folder above the other.

## iPad

- Home has its own **iPad** section in Settings → Home Screen. **Upright** and **On Its Side** can each be **One Column**, **Two Columns**, **Two Homes Side by Side** or, on its side, **In a Card**. **Search Bar** puts **Search All Servers** across the top. In a narrow Split View or Slide Over window, Home is one column.
- **Home Buttons in the Tab Bar** moves **+** and **Edit** into the tab bar.
- A book shows two pages side by side upright as well as on its side, because **Two Pages When Upright** is on to start with on an iPad.
- **Large Settings** (Settings → Appearance) makes Settings fill most of the screen. Off, it's a smaller card.
- A video fills the width, and everything else works the same as on an iPhone.

## What went wrong

| What you see | Try this |
|---|---|
| A setting from this page isn't there | It's for another device, or it's advanced. Turn on **Show Advanced Settings**. |
| The book shows one page, upright | Turn on **Two Pages When Upright** in Settings → Reading → **Page Layout**. |
| Settings is one column on an unfolded Duo | It's partly folded like a book, or **Two Columns on the Unfolded Duo** is off. |
| Home won't use two columns | Check Settings → Home Screen → **Unfolded, Upright** or **On Its Side**. |
